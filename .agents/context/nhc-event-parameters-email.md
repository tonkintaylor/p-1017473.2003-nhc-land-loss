### Geography/Exposure

- We were thinking that the boundary should be led by the type of land damage to
  have a cross section of land damage types rather than limiting it by TLA.
- Include areas by expected land-failure type e.g. steep hillside suburbs,
  reclaimed/harbour-edge land, alluvial land, coastal escarpment etc.
- Ideally the above will include areas where the most severe damage would occur,
  as well as the highest repair cost.
- From an equity lens, we are also interested in areas where damage would be
  widespread but individually low-cost to repair (given the implications for claim
  volumes and handling expense).
- If possible, areas where properties may be inaccessible due to infrastructure
  failure (roading/stair access etc) as this may drive up repair costs.
- Areas where multi-unit buildings, cross-lease and shared land as well as single
  standalone properties (to demonstrate impacts where there are multiple sub-caps
  for land structures and how splitting settlement between multiple insured
  interest may be impacted).

### Profile of insurance

- Residential properties
- Assume ~90% insurance penetration (based on NHC's estimate of 89-97%)
