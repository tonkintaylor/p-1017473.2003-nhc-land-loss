The Canterbury liquefaction costs are in 2010/2011 dollars. 1.42 is a placeholder: the growth in construction labour costs from the March 2011 quarter (1,030) to the December 2025 quarter (1,465) in the Stats NZ Labour Cost Index (salary and ordinary time wage rates, construction, series LCIQ.SG51E9). It follows pay rates, not plant or materials.
Yellow fields are the policy settings; blue fields scale the model's own estimates, to see how far an answer leans on them. The wall rates set both what a wall costs to replace and what it was worth, since both come from the same rate. Which properties are insured is not known, so that share of them, chosen at random but fixed per property, is insured; the rest are paid nothing and are not claims. Every figure is GST-inclusive at 15%. Everything else in the CSV is fixed by exposure and vulnerability.
