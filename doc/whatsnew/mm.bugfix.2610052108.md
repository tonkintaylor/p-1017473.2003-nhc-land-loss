One rule now decides which title stands for a stack of unit titles with identical geometry: `landloss.exposure.land.extent.stack_representatives` picks the lowest `source_id`. `build_claim_properties`, `gen_property_wall_records` and `property_of_pifs` all use it, so a stack's pifs, its claim and NZMM record, and its claim all go to the same title. A GNS-only wall piece takes its property by the pif rule, on length (`rateable_property_id`: the non-road property holding most of it, ties to the lowest id).
