Liquefaction land costs, which come from Canterbury in 2010/2011 dollars, are now brought to today's by a placeholder factor of 1.42 (`LIQ_COST_ESCALATION` in `landloss.loss.pricing`) before GST: the growth in the Stats NZ construction Labour Cost Index from the March 2011 to the December 2025 quarter (L-23, L-74). The loss viewer opens on the same factor as an editable field, with a note on where it comes from, and its histogram lays the land cap labels out in rows so they no longer print over each other.
