Each separate landslide on a property is now held by its own wall (T-124). Vul landslide land step 3 lists each failure that reached an insured land polygon -- its id, the retaining wall it failed with if any, the ground it took and its inundated area and depth -- and vul step 10 carries the lists into the loss land table (`landslide_ids`, `landslide_rw_ids`, `landslide_areas`, `landslide_inundated_areas`, `landslide_inundated_depths`). Loss step 1 holds a slip that came with a wall by that wall's replacement, sized against that slip alone, and gives every other slip a new wall of its own, even on a claim whose wall failed elsewhere. The settlement gains `new_wall_count`, `new_wall_face_m2` and `new_wall_rate_excl_gst_nzd_per_m2`; the viewer and the review spreadsheet sum a claim's new walls. A land table without the lists pools each claim's ground as one slip, as before. Rerun vul landslide land step 3, vul step 10 and loss.
