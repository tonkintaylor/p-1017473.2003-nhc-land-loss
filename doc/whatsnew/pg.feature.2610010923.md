**A claim's total damaged land counts the main access way.** The summary
table gives land within 8 m of the dwelling and land on or supporting the main
access way in separate blocks, and a claim can have its damage in either or
both: 1501000.0008 has all of it on the access way. `reports.csv` gains
`total_evacuated_m2`, `total_inundated_m2` and the three `total_imminent_*`
columns, each the sum of the two blocks.
