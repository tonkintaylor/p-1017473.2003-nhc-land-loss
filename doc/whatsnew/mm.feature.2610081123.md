A fill bank with no wall now fails on the same slip plane as a wall's face,
from its toe to the back of its width, and no deeper than its fill. It used to
take the whole fill thickness. Urban runout is capped at 2, 3 or 4 heights
depending on the ground below the toe: under 20°, 20° to 35°, or steeper. The
cut runout relation now reads the face angle with the 1 m DEM's cell of run
taken off, and measures its run past the toe from the face itself.
