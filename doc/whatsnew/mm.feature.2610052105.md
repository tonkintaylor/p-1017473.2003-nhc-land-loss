Retaining wall exposure step 6 reads the wall units of landslide step 12 in place of the candidate wall lines. `gen_wall_probability.py` (new `use_cached_layers` setting) ties each unit to its claim (`claim_of_properties`, so stacked unit titles share one claim) and puts the condition probability on it (`gen_unit_probability_table`). A unit with no height read is sized small, not large. `gen_wall_population.py` takes which units are walls from step 12's draw for each world (`draw_wall_population(..., walled=...)`, with the condition stream unchanged) and stops with a clear message if step 12 has not been run or a world was not drawn. Landslide step 12 is in no orchestrator yet, so run its faces and wall units scripts before `gen_exposure.py`.
