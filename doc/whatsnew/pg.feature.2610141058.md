The loss viewer's scenario values nothing: each claim is paid the lesser of its repair cost and its cap. With the sub-caps on, the repair is split three ways and each part limited on its own, per dwelling: the existing retaining walls with their share of the on-costs by the retaining wall sub-cap, the bridges and culverts with theirs by the bridge and culvert sub-cap, and the rest of the repair (land, liquefaction, spoil and the walls built to hold landslide ground) by the land cap. Room left under one limit does not pay for another part, so a scenario with sub-caps can pay less than the same land cap without them. A selected claim shows what each limit pays of the part it limits, and the summary shows what the scenario's sub-caps pay of the structures' repair. Rebuild the viewer to issue the page.
