A reader for ArcGIS REST services, `get_arcgis_feature_layer`, which pages
through a service the way councils publish data when it is not on Koordinates,
and `get_slide_interpreted_materials` on top of it. That layer is GNS Science's
SLIDE mapping of what the ground surface of urban Wellington is actually made
of — 9,215 polygons at nominally 1:500, from rock at or near the surface
through colluvium, talus, loess, alluvium and fill. It is the finest material
mapping the study can reach, though it covers only 38% of Wellington City and
none of the other three territorial authorities.
