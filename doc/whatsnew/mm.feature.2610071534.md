Urban slope runout now follows Hunter and Fell's (2003) travel angles from the
crest. Where the ground below a face's toe is 20° or steeper, it uses their
unconfined natural-slope relation on that angle, so debris runs on down steep
slopes. Otherwise it uses their cut relation on the face's angle. This
replaces the de Vilder et al. dry reach angle, which stopped almost every
pilot polygon at the 1 m strip and spread its debris back over its own scar.
The zones carry `source_angle_deg` and `downslope_angle_deg`, and `style` is
now `cut_slope` or `downslope`.
