The loss calculation walkthrough uses the viewer's definition of a claim: a property with damage, meaning a wall to replace or build, a crossing, or liquefaction worse than the None state. The Overview counts insured properties and claims separately and builds its headline numbers and charts from claims only, and the claim types and the twenty worked claims follow the same rule, so a flat property in the None state is no longer read as a liquefaction claim. GST is shown as fixed at 15% rather than as an input.
