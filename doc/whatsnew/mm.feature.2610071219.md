The localised (no wall) urban fragility is now calibrated the way the project lead chose on 2026-10-07 (`landloss.hazard.landslide.urban.area_calibration.fit_record_and_polygons`). It is fitted to the 2016 Kaikōura record of no urban failures at 0.15 g on rock (A16, an expected damaged share of 0.001 of the non-flat ground, weighted as much as the polygon anchors together) and to the GNS cut and fill polygon anchors A17 to A21. Kingsbury's High scenario 2 share (A12) is now only an upper limit the curve must stay under (`within_upper_limit`). The Kingsbury area fits stay in `table_urban_area_calibration.py` and `fig_urban_area_calibration.py` as rejected alternatives. The fitted constants are not yet adopted: they wait for a run on the rebuilt pilot.
