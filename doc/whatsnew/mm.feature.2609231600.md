The insured land extent now covers flats and cross-leases. One building outline
is shared with every address standing on it, and addresses on the same
coordinate become one property carrying a dwelling count, which is what NHC's
per-dwelling sub-caps and excess multiply. Over the Wellington pilot this takes
coverage from 4,764 of 8,591 addresses to 5,765 properties of 6,351, covering
7,862 dwellings, with the extents still pairwise disjoint.
