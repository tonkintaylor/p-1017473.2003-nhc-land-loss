Landslide step 12 places the retaining walls on the pifs. `gen_urban_slope_wall_units.py` joins the candidate pifs and GNS-only pieces of each property into wall units and puts a probability on each: a prior, the GNS floor, and an update on the walls the claim reports list and on NZMM, which is flagged unreliable. A seeded 30% of claimed properties is held out. It writes the unit table and one draw per exposure world. `gen_urban_slope_wall_zones.py` builds the evacuated, imminent and inundated zones of each world's draw; the every-siz-walled and none-walled runs stay as bounds. `table_urban_slope_wall_checks.py` writes four aggregate tables: GNS recall, the held-out claims, the strata, and the pilot counts with the height shape against Anderson et al. Any claim value over fewer than five properties is suppressed. Run settings are in the step's `config.py` (`WALL_JOIN_GAP_M`, `CLAIM_HOLDOUT_SHARE`, `WORLD_IDS` and others). `gen_property_wall_records` gives a stack of unit titles one record and carries `gns_walls_2m`.
