Give PGA per 100 m cell at each cell's TS1170.5 site class from the Foster et al. (2019) Vs30 model, in a new shaking step `hazard/shaking/steps/s4_pga_realisation` that reads the `s2_site_class` grid and keeps the per-realisation 10% scaling and the `beta-pga-rNNN` outputs. TS1170.5 PGA grids per site class and return period are generated from Table 3.2 by `static_data_gen/gen_ts1170_grids.py` (renamed from `gen_ts1170_sa_t1.py`, now writing Sa(1.0 s) and PGA) and read with `landloss.io.ts1170.get_ts1170_pga`; at 2500 years and site class 5 they equal the NLM grid cell for cell. At 2500 years PGA is lower on softer ground (non-linear site response), and most of the study area is Class II, so PGA rises over most of it against the old site class 5 field.
