The loss viewer counts a property as a claim only where it has damage: a damaged or new retaining wall, a crossing, or liquefaction worse than the None state. Claims the excess reduces to $0 are still counted. Flat properties in the None state, which carry the Canterbury average cost but have nothing to claim for, and hill properties no hazard reached are left out of the histogram, the share of claims above each land cap and the table of how claims settle; no total changes. The viewer CSV carries each claim's liquefaction state (liq_state), and has_liquefaction now means a state worse than None. The map shows claims by default, with every insured property as an option.
