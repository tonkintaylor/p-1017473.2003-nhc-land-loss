Retaining wall fragility is now picked by two height classes, under 2 m and 2 m and over (from each wall's `height_m`; unknown is under 2 m), instead of the three size classes, which stay on every wall for pricing. `retaining-wall-type-fragility.csv` is keyed on `(wall_type, height_class)` with a `height_effect` column: gravity masonry, old timber pole, block or RC cantilever and landscaper timber take the Koutsoupaki et al. (2023) height effect switched (6 m curve under 2 m, 3 m curve at 2 m and over), and crib, new timber pole and engineered modern take the 3 m curve for both. `wall_type_curves` and `wall_type_failure_probability` take `height_m` in place of `size_class`. The two wall fragility research figures draw the height classes and add the no-wall urban slope curve per Kingsbury zone in green.
