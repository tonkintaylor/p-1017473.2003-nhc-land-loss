The loss viewer has an insurance penetration setting, 90% by default (L-10): that share of properties, chosen at random but fixed per property, is insured, and the rest are paid nothing and are not counted as claims. The map's "All properties" view shows uninsured properties in dark grey. The land cap scenarios are now $30,000 (low), $65,000 (medium), $100,000 (high) and $150,000 (higher), each named wherever it appears.
