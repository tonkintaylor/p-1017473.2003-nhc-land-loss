`urban_area_calibration_findings.md` records the first area calibration of the localised urban fragility (wlg-pilot, 2026-10-07). The agreed fit (0.334 to 0.0668 m/s, beta 1.01) cannot reach Kingsbury's High share at scenario 2, because the bare footprints cover only 20% of the non-flat ground. It also fails the Kaikōura, Moderate, polygon and wall checks, so the placeholder median is kept until the project lead decides.
