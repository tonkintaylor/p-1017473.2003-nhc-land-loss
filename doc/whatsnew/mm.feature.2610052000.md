Added the pif cut and fill research (`src/scripts/landloss/hazard/landslide/research/cut_fill/`): `gen_pif_cut_fill.py` classes every urban slope pif as cut, fill, cut and fill or natural ground by comparing its crest and the foot of its face against two estimates of the natural surface (a 30 m rolling mean and a quadratic fitted around the pif), and `fig_pif_cross_sections.py` draws 100 m sections down the hill showing the DEM, both surfaces and the pips by class. Findings are in `pif_cut_fill.md`.
