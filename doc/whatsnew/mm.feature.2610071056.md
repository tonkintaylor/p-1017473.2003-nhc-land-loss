The urban fragility anchors now say what each fraction measures (`measure`). For the Kingsbury zone anchors it is the damaged share of the zone's non-flat area; for A17 to A21 it is the share of matching polygons failing, selected by `applies_to`, slope and `material`. A17's Port Hills demand is marked as recorded on site (`demand_at`). A15 (very severe) is 0.4: the lead set this because about a tenth of that damage comes from large landslides. A new `landloss.hazard.landslide.urban.area_calibration` computes the expected damaged share of the non-flat ground from the bare polygons' evacuated and inundated zones on 2 m cells, and fits the localised median's scale and dispersion to those shares. `table_urban_area_calibration.py` and `fig_urban_area_calibration.py` under `hazard/landslide/validations/urban/` run the fit and its checks.
