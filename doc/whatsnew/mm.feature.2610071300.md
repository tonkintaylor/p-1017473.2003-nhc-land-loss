A landslide reviewer page (`src/scripts/landloss/reviewer/gen_reviewer_landslide.py`) walks through both landslide populations in one self-contained HTML file, beside the retaining wall one. It covers the large failures (terrain, ground map, slope units, the Hancox and Kritikos coverage, and step 1's draw) and the urban failures (faces, zones, the per-polygon fragility and step 9's combined draw), then the land damage. Each step has maps cut from the current outputs, including a whole-pilot site for the slope-unit-scale steps. The page also has pilot-wide charts and 59 numbered assumptions (LS-A01 onwards), 13 of them marked where the code and the written status disagree. The shared template gains raster overlays, landslide layer styles and a PGV axis for fragility curves.
