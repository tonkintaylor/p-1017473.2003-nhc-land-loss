The liquefaction land damage probabilities are split into six states by ratios read off the Canterbury exceedance curves (T-88), in place of even placeholder shares: Minor takes a tenth of the None-to-Minor band rather than half, and the Major band splits by exceedance, P(at least Severe) two thirds and P(Very severe) a fifth of P(at least Major), giving Major 1/3, Severe 7/15 and Very severe 1/5. Over the study area the mean probability of Minor falls from 30% to 6%.
