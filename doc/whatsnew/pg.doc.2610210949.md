Start the appendices to the technical report, compiled on their own from `report/technical/appendices.typ` since the main report is written in Word. They describe the method for readers who have not seen the code, with no file names or register numbers. The first, the calculation of liquefaction damage, takes in the liquefaction hazard section from `technical_report.typ`, with lateral spreading as a subsection, and adds how a property's land damage state becomes evacuated, inundated and damaged ground. Its numbers come from a new script, `vul/liquefaction/land/report/gen_report_numbers.py`, over all four territorial authorities.
