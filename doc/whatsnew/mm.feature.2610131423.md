A four-panel map of the whole chain for the exec report, `post_processing/exec_report/fig_exec_chain_map.py`, written to `report/exec/chain/fig/exec-chain-map.png`. It shows one 600 by 450 m window south of the Porirua pilot four times: what is insured, what the earthquake does to the ground, what breaks, and the repair cost per item on one colour scale. The loss module's per-claim costs are split over the claim's walls, crossings, landslide ground and land; they are repair costs before the cap and excess. A new extent, `porirua-exec-map`, is the window with a 500 m margin, so the chain can be run over just that ground; until it has been, the figure draws the window from the whole-Porirua run.
