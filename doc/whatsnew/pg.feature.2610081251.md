The insured land extent judges a building a dwelling by what the QV rating roll says the property it stands on is used for, whatever the building's size (`RESIDENTIAL_RULE = "qv"` in step 5's config, now the default). Apartment blocks on residential land are kept, and shops, workshops and offices under 500 m² on commercial or industrial land are dropped. Buildings LINZ names are dropped as before, and a property the roll does not hold, or holds as vacant, falls back on the footprint rule. `RESIDENTIAL_RULE = "footprint"` keeps the earlier rule, which needs nothing from T:. The new `validations/gen_building_classification.py` writes every outline in the study area with its class and the evidence beside it for viewing in QGIS, and compares the two rules when run with the roll.
