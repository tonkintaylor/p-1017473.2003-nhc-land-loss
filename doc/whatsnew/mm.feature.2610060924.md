Wall units (landslide step 12) now read landslide step 13. A pif's wall height is the 80th percentile of its pips' drops to the foot of the face (`WALL_HEIGHT_QUANTILE`), not its largest pip drop, which put too few walls under 1.5 m; `max_delta_h_m` stays on the unit for reference. The prior reads each unit's cut and fill class (its longest pif's) in place of the ground map's fill: fill and cut and fill are raised, the rock reduction applies only to a cut in rock over 2.5 m, natural ground is lowered (`BETA_NATURAL_WALL_FACTOR`), and uncertain and unknown are neutral. `gen_urban_slope_wall_units.py` stops if step 13's tables are missing or older than the siz table, and `gen_hazard.main` runs step 13 between step 12's faces and wall units.
