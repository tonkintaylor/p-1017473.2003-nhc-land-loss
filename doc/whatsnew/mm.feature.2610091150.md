Ground step 3 joins only the pips within `BUILDING_REACH_M` (100 m) of a building outline into pifs, so a pif stops at the edge of the reach rather than being kept or dropped whole. On the Porirua pilot this keeps 2% more pifs and sizs and 11% more element area. Over a tiled extent each tile is searched only on the bounds of its buildings grown by the reach and `TILE_CROP_PAD_M` (100 m), which gives the same pifs as the whole tile, and the ground map and coastline are cut to each tile before they are burned. Together these took rural Upper Hutt tiles from several minutes to a few seconds. Landslide step 4 prints how many wall units with no element lie on no searched tile.
