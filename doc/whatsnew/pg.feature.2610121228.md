Liquefied land repair rates are now back-calculated so a 500 m2 section costs Virginie Lacrosse's Canterbury claimant-only median in each damage state, the per-claim spread fitted to her quartiles holding the median, and checked against Lower Hutt's claims in today's dollars. The Student Volunteer Army is modelled as clearing the first 250 m2 of each site's ejecta for nothing, the rest paid at the fitted ejecta rate; without volunteers all of it is paid, and the viewer gains a switch for this that needs no rerun. The None damage state costs nothing. The repair rate figures are redrawn for the new method.
