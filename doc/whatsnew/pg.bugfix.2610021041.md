**A property has one driveway, and only its first 60 m is insured land.**
Driveways were routed from every building on a property, so a second dwelling,
a garage or a shed each added an access way, and a route ran up to 300 m to the
road in full. The Act covers the main access way within 60 m of the dwelling. A
driveway is now routed once per property, from its largest building, and the
corridor covers the first 60 m of the route from the dwelling. The full route
length is still carried as `driveway_length_m` for the loss module's
construction access rating, beside a new `insured_driveway_length_m` (T-60,
T-61).
