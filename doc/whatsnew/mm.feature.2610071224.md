An urban slope deposit is now also never deeper than twice the failure's own evacuated depth (`BETA_MAX_DEPOSIT_DEPTH_SOURCE`), as well as never deeper than its height, before it spreads back over its own scar (`landloss.hazard.landslide.slope_polygons.deposit_overlap_m2`), so a shallow failure on a high face no longer piles its debris ten or more times its source depth in the strip at its toe. The forced wall polygons take the same limit.
