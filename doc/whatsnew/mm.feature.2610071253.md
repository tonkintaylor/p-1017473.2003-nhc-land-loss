The retaining wall reviewer page now draws a cross-section of every pif at its example sites, so a reviewer can see why landslide step 13 called a face cut, fill or natural. It draws the 1 m DEM, the quadratic surface step 13 fitted and saved for the pif (evaluated, not refitted), the pif's pips near the line, and the crest and foot residuals as bars above or below the surface, beside the medians the class actually rests on. It is drawn at true scale where it fits and labels its vertical exaggeration otherwise. A card under step 2's map shows the section of any pif clicked, and the trace panel shows the section of the traced unit's pif.
