The loss viewer compares a scenario with the Act. A new panel at the top designs the scenario: a fixed land cap per dwelling ($30,000, $65,000, $100,000 or $150,000, or any other amount), retaining wall and bridge and culvert sub-caps on top of it (lower, current Act, higher, or any other amounts) or none, so land structures are paid from within the cap, and its own excess (the Act's, a fixed $5,000, $10,000 or $15,000, or any other). The left-hand panel now sets only the baseline, the NHI Act 2023 as it stands, apart from the damage, cost and sensitivity assumptions, which move both. The summary gives the baseline and the scenario side by side, with the share of claims the scenario leaves better off, worse off and unchanged; the scenario is a row in each land cap table; the histogram, the land cover cap chart and the map each switch between baseline and scenario; the map can be coloured by whether a claim is better or worse off; and a selected claim is shown under both, line by line. The fixed land caps are now applied per dwelling, and neither they nor the scenario pay an undamaged property. Rebuild the viewer to issue the page.
