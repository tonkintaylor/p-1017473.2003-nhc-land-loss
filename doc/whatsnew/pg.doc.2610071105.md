A technical report is started in `report/technical/`, in Typst in the style of the NLM report, with a first section on the liquefaction hazard (T-70). Every number it states is read from YAML written by `hazard/liquefaction/report/gen_report_numbers.py`, and its limitations and work under way are read by ID from the register. Build it with `typst compile --root . report/technical/technical_report.typ`. Three liquefaction limitations from the step method files are added to the register (L-52 to L-54), and PyYAML is now a declared dependency.
