Added landslide step 13, pif cut and fill (`src/scripts/landloss/hazard/landslide/steps/s13_pif_cut_fill/`): every pif of step 12 is classed as cut, fill, cut and fill, uncertain or natural ground by comparing its crest and the foot of its face against a robust quadratic fitted to the ground off the faces (`landloss.hazard.landslide.pif_cut_fill`), so the retaining wall prior can treat walls as less likely on cuts, particularly in rock. The method from the cut and fill research is optimised from about 45 s to under 10 s over the Wellington pilot with the same classes, and the research scripts now run the library code.

The cut and fill research adds that third surface beside the rolling mean and the plain quadratic, with an uncertain class where a face is within the fit's own scatter, and its cross-section figure now shades where the ground stands above (fill) or below (cut) each pif's surface.
