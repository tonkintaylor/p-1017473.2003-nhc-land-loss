The urban slope model (landslide steps 5 and 6) and the flat-land retaining walls (vul shaking rw step 9) now evaluate every fragility on site PGA in g, the measure the wall curves are published on, instead of converting them to PGV. Landslide step 6 and vul shaking rw step 9 sample shaking step 4's PGA field; the model file's `im` is `pga_g`, its medians are in g, and the `theta_base_pga_g` and `pgv_pga_ratio_m_s_per_g` columns are gone, as are the steps' `RETURN_PERIOD_YR` settings. The draws and the combined realisation carry `pga_g` instead of `pgv_m_s`, and step 9 writes `pga_g` and `theta_base`. On the Porirua pilot every failure and wall outcome matched the PGV run. `fig_urban_fragility_curves.py` now draws one figure, on site PGA, against the study's 2,500-year site PGA range of 0.93 to 2.02 g.
