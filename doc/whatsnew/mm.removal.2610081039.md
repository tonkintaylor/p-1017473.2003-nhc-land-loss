Removed the old urban slope candidate method, which step 12 had replaced and nothing downstream read. Landslide steps 6 (urban slope candidates) and 7 (urban slope polygons) are gone, with their library code, figures and tests. Exposure rw step 6 no longer builds candidate wall lines; the wall population is drawn from step 12's wall units. Step 3 no longer writes the face height, profile curvature, 20 m topographic position or vegetation height layers, or fetches the 1 m DSM. A pipeline run skips two steps and a large part of the 1 m terrain work.
