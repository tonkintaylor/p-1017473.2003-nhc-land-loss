The loss viewer's CSV now types a claim by where its landslide ground came from. Ground that came down with the claim's own damaged retaining wall counts as retaining wall failure, not landslide, so a claim whose only landslide ground came with its own wall reads as retaining wall only rather than Combination. Ground from a neighbour's wall or a wall-less slope still counts as landslide. `cause` and `has_landslide` follow, and the new `wall_landslide_m2` column holds the ground that came down with the claim's own walls.
