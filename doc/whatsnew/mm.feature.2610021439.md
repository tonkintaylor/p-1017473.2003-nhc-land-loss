Read QV's rating roll for the four Wellington councils with `landloss.io.qv_rating_roll.get_qv_rating_roll`: one row per rating unit, 167,369 in all, with land and capital value, land area, building age decade and QPID. The files carry no header, so the 67 fields are named by position, with the open LINZ valuation roll's column names wherever the values line up and `field_NN` where the meaning is unknown; a `README.md` beside the files records the evidence. Dollars, counts and areas come back as nullable numbers, the Y/N flags as booleans and the valuation date as a date. The files sit under `SourceMaterial/SENSITIVE` on T: (`QV_RATING_ROLL_SOURCE_DIR`, `QV_RATING_ROLL_FILES`) and are destroyed, with their local cache copies, at the end of the project.
