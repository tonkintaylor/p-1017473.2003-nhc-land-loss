The weekly update now shows the plan with progress marked against it rather than
a prose status. Each `Approach` bullet in a `status.md` carries a mark — `[x]`
done, `[~]` partly done, `[>]` next, `[ ]` planned — and the update's first table
row renders those directly as ✓, italics, ▸ and a grey dot, with a legend beside
the date. `What's next` stays a numbered list, since marks carry no ordering.

Because the plan is stable week to week, the marks moving are the progress, and
the diff between two updates is what changed. `hazard/liquefaction/status.md`
splits its plan under **Prototype** and **Beyond prototype**;
`hazard/landslide/status.md` stays unmarked while its route is undecided.
