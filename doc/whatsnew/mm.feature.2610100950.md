The ground map's polygons now follow bodies of cut, fill and groundwater class rather than the 1 m and 100 m cells. Ground step 2 classes the NLM groundwater depth first (saturated, poorly drained, well drained) and polygonises the class, reads the 30 m residual at 10 m, sieves the natural, cut and fill classes once so a patch under 900 m² takes its largest neighbour's class, and simplifies both sets of polygons as a coverage so shared edges stay shared. The map carries `gw_depth_class` and no longer carries `gw_depth_m`; the urban chain and the QGIS and reviewer outputs read the class, so a ground map built before this change must be rebuilt (ground step 2). A depth exactly on a break (1.0 m or 3.0 m) takes the deeper class, as `groundwater_value()` does, so both routes score it alike. Simplification can leave a polygon slightly under the minimum patch area. The fill thickness is read from the 1 m residual in full-width strips, which decodes each row once. The research script `research/slope_elements/gen_ground_map_qgis_layers.py` still calls the old signatures and no longer imports; it is research and left unmaintained.
