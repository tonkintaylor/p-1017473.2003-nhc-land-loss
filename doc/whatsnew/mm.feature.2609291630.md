Read the National Liquefaction Model's 2500-year PGA and Sa(1.0 s) grids at any of TS1170.5 site classes 1-7 (`landloss.io.nlm.get_nlm_scenario_pga_2500yr`, `get_nlm_scenario_sa_t1_2500yr`), convert Sa(1.0 s) to PGV with `landloss.hazard.shaking.pgv`, and default to site class 2 through `constants.BETA_SITE_CLASS` until the Foster et al. (2019) Vs30 model supplies the class per location. The Nowicki Jessee model now builds on a grid registered as the USGS's is and computes slope after resampling the DEM onto it, which brings its Loma Prieta total landslide area to within 1% of the USGS run.
