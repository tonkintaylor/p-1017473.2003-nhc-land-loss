The QV land tiers now value the rest of the insured land at 0.2 of the rate under the house and the rest of the section at 0.02, in place of 0.5 and 0.15. A valuer reviewing a 3,000 m² section worth $600,000 expected about $300,000 of its value under the house; the new ratios give $303,000 ($2,020/m²), against $120,000 before. Damaged land is now valued well above its section's average, most of all on large sections; on a typical 700 m² section about three quarters of the land value now sits under the house, which a valuer has yet to confirm (T-153).
