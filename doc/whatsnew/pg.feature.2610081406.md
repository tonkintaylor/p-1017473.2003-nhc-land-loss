The new `exposure/land/validations/gen_land_value_vs_qv.py` compares the modelled land value of every claim property against the QV rating roll, indexed to the same valuation date. It prints the ratio by territorial authority and landform, writes a four-panel figure to `report/exposure/land/land-value/fig/`, and writes suburb (and, locally, per-property) layers with a QGIS project to `temp/exposure/land-value-vs-qv/`.
