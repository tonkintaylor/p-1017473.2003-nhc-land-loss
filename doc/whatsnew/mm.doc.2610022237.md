**Stage D1 toy terrain figures and findings for the slope elements.**
`src/scripts/landloss/hazard/landslide/research/slope_elements/fig_toy_slope_elements.py`
runs the 12 toy cases (15 grids) through the slope elements and polygons, with and
without LiDAR-like noise, and draws each as a plan over hillshade and a true-scale
section in `report/hazard/landslide/slope-elements/fig/`. The expected and observed
outcomes, the noise runs and the open problems are in `toy_slope_elements.md` beside it.
