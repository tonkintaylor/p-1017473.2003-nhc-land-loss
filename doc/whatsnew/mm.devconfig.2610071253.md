A `building-reviewer-pages` skill in `.agents/skills/` records how the reviewer pages are built and extended. It covers the rules that keep a page honest while the model moves under it (show what the steps wrote and never recompute, take paths from the steps, report missing and stale outputs, store sites as coordinates and constants by name, keep assumption ids permanent). It also covers the folder layout, how to add a chain, a map layer, a chart or a cross-section, the headless Chrome check for console errors, and the mistakes made building the first page.
