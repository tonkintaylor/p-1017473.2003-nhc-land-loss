The exec chain map (`fig_exec_chain_map.py`) now draws one figure per entry in the exec report config's `CHAIN_WINDOWS`, as the liquefaction chain map does: Porirua's 600 by 450 m window, still written to `exec-chain-map.png`, and Alicetown and Trentham read out of the whole-city Lower Hutt and Upper Hutt runs, written to `exec-chain-map-lower-hutt-alicetown.png` and `exec-chain-map-upper-hutt-trentham.png`. A window can be an NZTM box or an extent's name, and the map panels take their height from its shape. The config's `EXTENT` setting is gone; each window names the extent it is read from.
