Landslide step 12's siz table now carries what the retaining wall units need to join pieces of one wall end to end: each pip's fall direction (`pip_direction`), each pif's spine (the longest path through the minimum spanning tree of its pips, `landloss.hazard.landslide.instability_zones.gen_pif_spines`) with its two ends, the fall direction at each end (the mean over the pips within `PIF_END_WINDOW_M`), how much it bends (`fall_resultant`), and the rateable property a wall on it belongs to (`rateable_property_id`, `rateable_share`: the property with most of its pips unless that is a road parcel, then the next). The GNS-only wall candidates are indexed by `gns_only_id`.
