New library module `landloss.hazard.landslide.wall_units` turns the step 12 retaining wall candidates into wall units, one per wall. It joins the candidate pifs and GNS-only pieces of a property end to end, through one GNS mapped wall feature or at a corner, and puts a probability on each unit. That probability is built from a prior (siz or small, height band, rock cut, fill), a GNS floor (0.95, or 0.8 for a GNS-only unit) and a Poisson-binomial update on the claim report and NZMM wall counts per property, with a seeded hold-out. Every probability rises or stays, and any shortfall is written out as candidates missing. The module also draws each unit walled per exposure world and turns a draw into the element flags the polygon builder reads. Its judgement weights are new `BETA_` constants in `landloss.domain.constants`, beside a `WALL_UNIT_ID_PREFIX` (`WU`). `BETA_ROCK_CUT_FACTOR` now lives there too, and `landloss.exposure.rw.wall_probability` reads it from there.
