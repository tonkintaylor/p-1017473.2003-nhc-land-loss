The urban slope chain now runs on the walls each exposure world drew. Landslide step 8 reads landslide step 12's zones of each world's wall draw (`urban-slope-zones-wNNN`) in place of step 7's polygons, through the new `landloss.hazard.landslide.urban.face_polygons`: a polygon's wall is its element's wall unit, the same unit exposure rw step 6 exposes as that world's wall, its geometry and depths are the world's own, and its Kingsbury rating is scored from its element and the ground map (`BETA_OFF_MAP_GROUND` off the map). Step 8 stops if the zones and the drawn walls are not one draw (`check_zones_match_walls`), replacing the guard that stopped every run on unit ids against step 7's lines, so `gen_all.py` runs end to end again. Step 9 is unchanged and groups each wall unit's polygons under one draw. `gen_hazard.main_urban` no longer runs step 7, and step 8's `WORLD_IDS` follows exposure rw step 6. For the pilot figures, step 12's new `fig_urban_slope_wall_zones.py` draws the every-siz-walled and no-siz-walled zones side by side at the pilot sites (`FIG_ZONE_SCENARIOS` in step 12's config; add `"w000"` for a world's drawn walls).
