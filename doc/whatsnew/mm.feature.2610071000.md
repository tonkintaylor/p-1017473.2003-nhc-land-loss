Every wall candidate now has its minimum evacuated polygon (the lead, 2026-10-07). A GNS-only or low-height candidate whose line finds no free cell for a line element (every cell already another element's, or off the DEM) gets the band `max(0.5 H, 1 m)` behind its line drawn as geometry on the uphill side read off the DEM (centred and flagged `side_unknown` where the DEM cannot tell), with the line element's depth and volume, an imminent band and an inundated strip on level ground, overlapping other polygons where it must (`landloss.hazard.landslide.forced_polygons`). Its element and zones are added to step 12's elements and zones files (`forced`), so steps 8 and 9 treat it as any other polygon; step 9's absorb rule keeps shared ground from being counted twice.
