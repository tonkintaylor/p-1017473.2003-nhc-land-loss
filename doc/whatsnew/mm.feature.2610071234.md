A reviewer page for the retaining wall chain. `src/scripts/landloss/reviewer/gen_reviewer_rw.py` writes one self-contained HTML file, `report/reviewer/rw/rw-reviewer-pilot.html`, that walks from the pips on the DEM to the damage flags the loss module reads. It has an overview with a d3 flow diagram, the inputs and their licences, and one section per step. Each step section carries its narrative, the files it reads and writes with their dates and row counts, a Leaflet map of six example sites over a 1 m hillshade, pilot-wide charts, its key tables and its numbered assumption boxes (`RW-A01` to `RW-A32`). Clicking a wall unit opens a side panel that traces that one wall from its probability stages to its outcome and flags. The page recomputes nothing: every layer is cut from the current outputs. A freshness panel flags any output older than the outputs it was built from; on the first build it caught the vul flags, the loss input and the land damage still holding 1,754 walls against today's 1,398. The narrative, the assumptions and the sites live in three TOML files beside the script, and constants are written there by name and filled in from `landloss.domain.constants` at build time. The page carries claim ids, so `report/reviewer/` is gitignored.
