Urban slope model: pips, pifs and sizs (seed instability zones) replace the free-face and bank seeding. Cells that drop 0.7 m per metre at 1, 3 and 5 cells in any of eight directions are clustered within 2 m and each cluster is tested over all pairs against the two-band slope table and a near step, with fill taken as soil. The sizs are grown by the existing watershed growth, the evacuated zones are drawn with every siz walled and with none, and the siz table is saved for the retaining-wall workflow.
