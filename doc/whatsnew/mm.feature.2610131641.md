A pif wall unit's line is now drawn along the downhill stretch of its pif's outline with 0 to 3 bends (ground step 3, `wall_lines.py`, the `wall_line` column). Pips the line does not hold are split off as pif pieces of their own with candidate class `no_wall`, which get no wall unit. Landslide step 4 trims each walled element's evacuated polygon at its wall line and fills gaps between the polygon and the wall (`wall_trim.py`, `zone_alignment.py`).
