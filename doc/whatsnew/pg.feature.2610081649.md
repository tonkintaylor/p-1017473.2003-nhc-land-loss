Liquefaction land claims now vary in cost within a land damage state: each claim's fixed cost is drawn around its mean, with a spread fitted to the quartiles of Canterbury claimant-only costs, which are used as a calibration target only. Vul liquefaction land step 3 fits the spread and reports the modelled quartiles beside Canterbury's. The packaged Canterbury claimant figures carry the quartiles and unrounded means, and are read with `load_claimant_costs`.
