The loss viewer builds the land cover cap from each claim's damaged walls valued wall by wall, as the loss module does, rather than from one size, length and rate per claim. A claim with walls of mixed sizes had every metre priced at its tallest wall's size and highest rate, overstating its cap by up to $305,379 on the pilot; the viewer now agrees with the module to $0.23. Its CSV carries `wall_value_excl_gst` in place of `wall_face_m2` and `wall_rate_excl_gst`.
