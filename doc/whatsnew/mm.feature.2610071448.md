A landslide QGIS project is now built by a script (`src/scripts/landloss/hazard/landslide/validations/qgis/gen_landslide_qgis_project.py`) rather than by hand. It shows every urban polygon's evacuated, imminent and inundated ground, marked failed or not failed in the realisation, and the pips coloured by their pif's cut/fill class. It also carries the pif spines, the step 8 medians and wall states, the step 12 world and scenario zones, the large-failure coverage and slope units, and the ground map, contours and 1 m hillshade. The landslide reviewer page's zones step now has a table saying how evacuated, inundated and imminent land are set for a walled face and an unwalled one, with the pilot's medians per rule.
