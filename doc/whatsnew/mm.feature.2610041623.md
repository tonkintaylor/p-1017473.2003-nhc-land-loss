Reproduced the Kritikos et al. (2015) success-rate AUCs on the paper's own events (`validations/kritikos_2015/`): Wenchuan 0.831 against the paper's 0.839, and Northridge 0.867 to 0.927 depending on the study area against 0.904, using Copernicus 30 m and GEM Global Active Faults as stand-ins for the paper's DEM and fault maps. The digitisation is good enough to use. Chi-Chi cannot be reproduced because the GFDB lacks its landslides. The study area moves the AUC more than any model choice, and the fault term lowers Wenchuan's AUC with the GEM faults.
