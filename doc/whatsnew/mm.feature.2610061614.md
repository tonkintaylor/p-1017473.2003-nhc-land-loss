Retaining walls now fail on the curve of their wall type rather than of their condition. Landslide step 8 (walls on sloping land) and vul shaking rw step 9 (walls on flat land) read `retaining-wall-type-fragility.csv`, one curve per wall type and size class on the moderate damage state, and scale its PGA median by the wall's own position (0.85 retaining fill, 1.15 retaining a cut) before converting it to PGV. The model file carries `wall_type` in place of `wall_class` and `initial_condition`, the flat-land damage state file carries `wall_type`, `age_bin` and `wall_position`, and `retaining-wall-fragility.csv` is retired.
