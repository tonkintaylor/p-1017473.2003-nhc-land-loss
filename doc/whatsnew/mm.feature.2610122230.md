The urban slope model's topographic amplification is now one factor, `TOPOGRAPHIC_AMPLIFICATION` = 1.3, for every polygon whose representative point is off NLM flatland, and 1.0 on it (`face_polygons.with_amplification` reads the ground map's `is_flatland`). It replaces the placeholder of up to 1.5 on the 100 m topographic position and the face's own slope, and landslide step 5 no longer reads the topographic position grid. On the Porirua pilot 925 of 1,681 polygons lie on NLM flatland and take 1.0; urban failures fell from 758 to 684 evacuated polygons and walls failed with their polygon from 172 to 154.
