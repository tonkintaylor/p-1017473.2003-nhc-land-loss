The retaining wall placement QGIS project is now built by a script (`src/scripts/landloss/exposure/rw/validations/qgis/gen_rw_qgis_project.py`) in place of a hand-run one. It opens with every potential wall (wall unit) coloured by its probability `p_wall`, in ten equal bins, and carries the prior, GNS floor and claim stages as attributes. It also shows the units by the stage that set `p_wall`, world 0's draw, the exposure walls by outcome, the urban ground by zone (failed or not), the pips by cut/fill and candidate class, the elements and scenario zones, and the GNS walls, property boundaries, contours, ground map and hillshade.
