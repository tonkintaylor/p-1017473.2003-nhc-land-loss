Landslide step 12's pif pieces and wall units may now turn no more than `MAX_TOTAL_TURN_DEG` (185 degrees) in all along their line: where they would, a new piece or wall starts (`bend_split`, one rule for both, and checked by `rule_breaks`). A line over 50 m is now cut first at its own bends (the fewest cuts that bring every part under 50 m, the most even of those), then, for walls only, at the property boundaries it crosses, then into equal pieces; `WALL_SPLIT_AT_BOUNDARY_OVER_M` is removed, the boundary stage being part of the 50 m cap (`WALL_MAX_LENGTH_M`).
