A retaining wall is now insured by the stretches of it that support a claim's insured land, not by touching the claim it was drawn on. Exposure rw step 6 cuts each drawn wall into stretches: the line inside a claim's property (LINZ parcel) and within 2 m of that claim's insured land. A wall with any stretch of 1 m or more is insured, with its `rw_id` on the claim with the longest stretch, so a wall that holds up a neighbour's land is insured on the neighbour. The stretches are written on each wall as `insured_lengths_m` and, with their lines, to `wall-insured-stretches-wNNN-<extent>.geoparquet`. Vul step 10 splits each wall on `insured_lengths_m`, one piece per claim at its own stretch length, dropping stretches under 1 m and no longer giving a primary piece the remainder, so length that supports no insured land is not priced. A wall on a shared property boundary is split equally between the claims whose land it supports, half the length each, and the 1 m minimum applies to the share.
