Estimate PGV across the study area in two new shaking steps. `hazard/shaking/steps/s2_site_class` assigns the TS1170.5 site class per 100 m cell from the Foster et al. (2019) Vs30 model (read with `landloss.io.vs30.get_foster_2019_vs30` and `get_foster_2019_vs30_sigma`), on a grid the PGA step can share. `hazard/shaking/steps/s3_pgv` gives each cell the TS1170.5 Sa(1.0 s) of its class and PGV in m/s at 750 mm/s per g (`landloss.hazard.shaking.pgv.pgv_m_s_from_sa_1s`), and `fig_pgv.py` maps site class and PGV. Site class is from Vs30 alone, without Table 3.3's profile criteria (register limitation L-38).
