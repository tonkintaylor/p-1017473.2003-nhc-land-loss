Urban slope runout now adds a seismic distance set by the polygon's Kingsbury
susceptibility zone: 0.5 m for very low and low, 1 m for moderate, 2 m for
high and 3 m for very high. The distances are set loosely for a Mw 7.5, 0.7 g
earthquake. The seismic distance is added to the travel-angle run, the total
is held to the height cap, and it replaces the 1 m strip every failure used to
leave. The zone is now scored in step 12, before the inundated zone is drawn.
