The no-wall urban slope fragility in landslide step 5 is now set on site PGA by the project lead, as a p15 and a p50 at two susceptibility ratings in the packaged `urban-localised-fragility.csv` (p15 0.7 and p50 2.0 g at zone 3, p15 0.3 and p50 0.6 g at zone 5). Both percentiles run in straight lines in log PGA against the rating, with the dispersion falling from 1.38 at zone 1 to 0.67 at zone 5, so low-susceptibility ground has a flatter curve. `assign_fragility` converts it to PGV at each polygon's own PGV/PGA ratio, as it does the wall curves, and now writes `theta_base_pga_g` and `pgv_pga_ratio_m_s_per_g` on every row. It replaces the placeholder PGV medians of 3.0 to 0.6 m/s with dispersion 0.6. `fig_urban_fragility_curves.py` draws the new curve.
