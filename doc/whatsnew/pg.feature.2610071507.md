The loss viewer is laid out for the land cap comparison (T-86, T-87). The main histogram shows claims by repair cost or by settlement under the Act on a log scale, with Bridget Attwood's land cap scenarios ($15k, $30k, $90k and $120k, editable through "Update cap settings") as vertical lines labelled with the share of damaged claims above each. A right-hand panel gives the total under the Act, how many claims settle on each basis, and each cap's total settlement and change from the Act, with a toggle for whether the cap replaces the Act's land cover cap or limits it. Clicking a claim on the map shows its intermediate values and its settlement under each cap, and the map can be filtered to paid or capped claims and its dot size changed. GST is fixed at 15% rather than being an input, the total cap input is replaced by the scenarios, and two sensitivity scales are added, on the retaining wall rates and on the land value.
