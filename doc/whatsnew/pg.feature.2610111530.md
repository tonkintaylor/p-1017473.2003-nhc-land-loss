The loss module now values damaged land itself, from each property's own land value on the QV rating roll (indexed to 1 September 2025) spread over three tiers: the building footprint at the full rate, the rest of the insured land at half and the uninsured remainder at 0.15. Landslide ground is valued by how much of it lies under the footprint, which vul now measures; liquefaction damage takes the insured land's average. A property not on the roll takes its suburb's median QV rate per m2. Exposure no longer writes a land rate and the land table no longer carries `$/m2 market value`; exposure s5, vul and loss need rerunning, and loss reads the roll from T:. See `src/scripts/landloss/loss/qv_land_value_method.md`.
